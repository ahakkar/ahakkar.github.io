Avatar concepts

Mockup for #40, repo only. Concept 3 was chosen; see spec section 6.9. Four ideas for the ahakkar avatar in the portfolio's colours. You upload a square and GitHub crops it to a circle, so every mark stays inside the circle. GitHub shows avatars as small as 20 px, so each concept is previewed in GitHub's light and dark themes at that size. The AH letters are optically centred: moved left and up so their ink, not their bounding box, is centred.

Why no font

The letters are drawn as shapes, not typed in a font. They are a heavy geometric sans, close to the weight-900 system font of the name on the site. Drawn letters look the same on every device, and there's no font licence to worry about. Installed fonts such as Aptos are licensed for documents, not logos.

For GitHub the avatar is exported as a 1024 × 1024 PNG. On the site it is a small SVG in img/, so it stays sharp at any size.
